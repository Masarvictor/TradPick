Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

TradPick is an internal content-publishing workspace operated by Masar Stores in Oman. This policy explains the limited information processed when an authorized creator connects a social account.

Information processed

  • Platform account identifiers and authorization tokens supplied through official OAuth flows.
  • Creator-selected video files, captions, publishing settings, and platform responses.
  • Basic operational logs needed to diagnose failed or successful publishing requests.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the creator, submit creator-approved content, check publishing status, prevent unintended duplicate processing, and maintain security.

Sharing and retention

Content and required account data are sent only to the platforms selected by the creator and to temporary media infrastructure required for platform ingestion. Temporary hosted media is scheduled for deletion after processing. TradPick does not sell personal information.

Security and revocation

Access tokens are treated as confidential. Creators can revoke platform access using the connected platform’s account settings. Revocation prevents future publishing requests, subject to platform processing already underway.

Your choices

An authorized creator may request deletion of operational data or stop using TradPick. Platform-hosted posts must be managed through the relevant platform.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the operator through the contact details supplied in the associated developer application.
